This project is an exploration of **animation, motion graphics, and visual storytelling**, focusing on movement, timing, transitions, and visual rhythm.

## Background

The project was created as an opportunity to experiment with animation beyond static visual design. I explored how movement and timing can influence the way an idea, object, or scene is perceived.

## Core Idea

The focus was on understanding the principles behind animation and experimenting with different forms of motion to create visually engaging sequences.

## Process

I experimented with **timing, spacing, keyframes, transitions, and motion** while developing the animation. The process involved testing different movements and refining them to create smoother and more intentional visual sequences.

## Motion Graphics & Advertising

Alongside animation experiments, I also worked on **motion graphics for advertising**, transforming static designs and promotional concepts into dynamic visual content.

These projects explored the use of **animated typography, graphic elements, transitions, imagery, and visual effects** to create engaging advertisements for digital platforms.
