Project Nyx is a sci-fi motorcycle concept and 3D visualization project created by me, exploring futuristic vehicle design through a restrained and minimal visual language.

## Background

With this design exploration, I focused on creating a futuristic sleeper-style motorcycle that emphasizes restraint over aggression. The form language is deliberately minimal, concealing high-performance engineering beneath a calm, low-profile silhouette.

The project explores how a motorcycle can communicate power and performance without relying on overly aggressive shapes or exposed mechanical components.

## Core Idea

Built around a carbon-fiber-dominant structure and oversized fat tires, the bike balances visual weight with functional stability. The design focuses on proportion, material, texture, and scale to create a strong yet refined appearance.

The overall concept is centered around the idea of hidden power existing within an understated futuristic form.

## Process

I created the motorcycle as a 3D model, developing its overall form, proportions, materials, and surface details. I focused on creating realistic materials and a coherent futuristic aesthetic while maintaining the minimal character of the design.

The final visualization combines detailed modeling, material work, lighting, and composition to present the motorcycle as a complete industrial design concept.
