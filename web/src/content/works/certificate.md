## Award

### Miscut — A Stop-Motion Film

*Miscut* is a collaborative stop-motion film project in which I contributed as a member of the **Set Design Team**. I worked on developing and arranging the physical environments used throughout the film, ensuring consistency in scale, composition, and visual detail.

The film received the **Award of Excellence by ASIFA India in 2022**, an **international recognition** presented for the project.

**Award:** Award of Excellence  
**Organization:** ASIFA India  
**Year:** 2022  
**Project:** *Miscut — A Stop-Motion Film*  
**Role:** Set Design Team
