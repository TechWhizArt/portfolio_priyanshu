A collection of advertising videos I created for different businesses, handling both the shooting and post-production.

## Background

I worked on advertisements for different businesses, adapting each video to its brand, audience, and promotional purpose.

## Process

I handled **video shooting, editing, pacing, transitions, text animation, visual effects, and sound synchronization** to create concise and engaging advertisements.
