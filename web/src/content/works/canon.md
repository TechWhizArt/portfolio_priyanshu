Double Barrel Plasma Cannon was my first 3D project, completed in November 2023 as an exploration of hard-surface modeling and detailed futuristic design.

## Background

With this creative exploration, I moved toward a darker, more industrial, futuristic, and high-impact art style. The project allowed me to move beyond standard design and build a coherent sci-fi environment centered around a large-scale mechanical weapon.

## Core Idea

The concept focuses on raw mechanical power, industrial complexity, and imposing scale. The double-barrel plasma cannon combines heavy structural elements with intricate mechanical details to create a powerful and futuristic visual identity.

The goal was to create a design that feels functional and ready for action while maintaining a strong visual presence.

## Process

I created the 3D model by building the weapon and its surrounding mechanical components from the ground up. I focused on hard-surface modeling, mechanical detailing, proportions, materials, and overall composition.

The final artwork combines detailed geometry, industrial materials, lighting, and scale to create a cohesive futuristic sci-fi environment.
